REMOTE SENSING COURSEWORK · LAND-COVER CHANGE

Land-use and land-cover change in Gaibandha

A coursework study using multi-date satellite imagery, land-cover classification, NDVI and change detection to examine land-cover conditions between 2016 and 2020.

Study area: Gaibandha UpazilaPeriod: 2016–2020Methods: classification · NDVI · change detection
On this page
QuestionWorkflowOutputsLimitations

Study question

The project examined how major land-cover classes changed between 2016 and 2020 and used vegetation indices and classification outputs to support the interpretation of those changes.

Workflow

01Prepare multi-date imagery
02Classify land cover
03Calculate NDVI
04Compare 2016 and 2020

The coursework included supervised and unsupervised classification outputs, class-area calculations and vegetation-index maps. The purpose was to learn how different remote-sensing products can be used together to describe spatial change.

Selected outputs

Limitations

Two-date change detection is sensitive to season, atmospheric conditions, class confusion and training data. The outputs are most useful as a coursework demonstration of classification and change-detection methods rather than as a complete long-term land-use history.
