REMOTE SENSING COURSEWORK · LAND-COVER CHANGE

Land-use and land-cover change in Gaibandha

In this coursework project, I used multi-date satellite imagery, land-cover classification, NDVI and change detection to examine land-cover conditions between 2016 and 2020.

Study area: Gaibandha UpazilaPeriod: 2016–2020Methods: classification · NDVI · change detection
On this page
QuestionWorkflowOutputsSpectral profilesLimitations

Study question

I examined how major land-cover classes changed between 2016 and 2020, using vegetation indices and classification outputs to interpret the change.

Workflow

01Prepare multi-date imagery
02Classify land cover
03Calculate NDVI
04Compare 2016 and 2020

I produced supervised and unsupervised classification outputs, class-area calculations and vegetation-index maps. The exercise helped me compare how different remote-sensing products describe spatial change.

Selected outputs

Landsat 5 spectral profiles (1992)

Landsat 5 spectral distributions for agriculture, barren land, built-up land, vegetation and water bodies, using a scene acquired on 8 March 1992.

Limitations

The 2016–2020 comparison uses two image dates; seasonal differences, atmospheric conditions and classification uncertainty affect the interpretation of change.
