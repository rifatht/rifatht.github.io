PUBLISHED WORK · GIS · REGIONAL RESILIENCE

Spatial flood vulnerability of Greater Rangpur

A co-authored GIS-based regional vulnerability assessment integrating socioeconomic and physical indicators to support resilience planning in northern Bangladesh.

Publication: ICURP 2021 / Bangladesh Institute of PlannersMethod: multi-indicator GIS vulnerability assessment
On this page
StudyFrameworkFindingsLimitations

Study purpose

The study assessed spatial flood vulnerability across Greater Rangpur to identify where socioeconomic sensitivity and physical exposure combine to create greater regional planning challenges. The analysis used GIS to integrate multiple indicators into a common spatial framework.

Indicator framework

The published work used 18 socioeconomic and physical indicators. Indicators were normalized and combined to produce comparable vulnerability scores and classes across the study area.

18 indicatorssocioeconomic and physical dimensions
Regional scaleGreater Rangpur, northern Bangladesh
GIS integrationspatial vulnerability classes for planning

Planning findings

The study identified marked spatial variation in vulnerability and linked higher scores to combinations of poverty, agricultural dependence, service and infrastructure gaps, low elevation and proximity to rivers. The value of the index is comparative: it shows where multiple disadvantage and exposure indicators overlap.

Limitations

Composite vulnerability indices depend on indicator selection, normalization and weighting. The source datasets also represent different dates. These choices should be considered when using the map for planning decisions or comparing it with later observations.
