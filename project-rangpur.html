Spatial flood vulnerability of Greater Rangpur

I co-authored this GIS-based assessment of regional flood vulnerability in northern Bangladesh, combining socioeconomic and physical indicators for resilience planning.

Publication: ICURP 2021 / Bangladesh Institute of PlannersMethod: multi-indicator GIS vulnerability assessment
On this page
StudyFrameworkFindingsLimitations

Study purpose

We assessed flood vulnerability across Greater Rangpur to identify where socioeconomic sensitivity and physical exposure overlap. We used GIS to integrate the indicators into a common spatial framework.

Indicator framework

We used 18 socioeconomic and physical indicators, normalized them and combined them into comparable vulnerability scores and classes across the study area.

18 indicatorssocioeconomic and physical dimensions
Regional scaleGreater Rangpur, northern Bangladesh
GIS integrationspatial vulnerability classes for planning

Planning findings

We found clear spatial variation in vulnerability. Higher scores were associated with combinations of poverty, agricultural dependence, service and infrastructure gaps, low elevation and proximity to rivers. The index lets us compare where several disadvantage and exposure indicators overlap.

Limitations

Composite vulnerability indices depend on indicator selection, normalization and weighting, and the source datasets represent different dates. Those limits matter when I interpret the map or compare it with later observations.
