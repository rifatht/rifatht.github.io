Sukundirbag Tower traffic impact assessment

An individual traffic-impact coursework project for a proposed office development in Gazipur, covering traffic counts, passenger-car-unit conversion, trip generation, background growth and site-access recommendations.

Project type: individual courseworkMethods: traffic counts · PCU · trip generation
On this page
Study setupTraffic surveyTrip generationFuture scenarioLimitations

Study setup

The proposed office development was assessed in relation to the nearby Bonomala Rail Gate junction and its connecting approaches. The coursework considered existing traffic conditions together with expected growth in office employment.

Traffic survey and PCU conversion

The survey recorded cycles, motorcycles, vans, rickshaws, CNGs, cars, buses and trucks and converted mixed traffic to passenger-car units so directional flows could be compared on a common basis.

Trip generation

The project estimated employee trip rates from observed access patterns and applied them to the planned increase in employment. This provided a project-generated demand component that could be compared with background traffic.

Future traffic scenario

The coursework included a five-year background-growth assumption and compared future directional demand with the capacity assumptions used in the report. The planning recommendations focused on access management, markings and junction operation.

Limitations

The analysis was a coursework-level traffic assessment based on a limited survey period and simplified capacity assumptions. A professional TIA would normally use repeated turning-movement counts, junction delay and queue analysis, separate trip-generation categories and explicit pedestrian, motorcycle and public-transport conditions.
