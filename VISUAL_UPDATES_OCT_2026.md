# Visual research update (October 2026)

The cyclone research page uses original figures from `UNDP_Report.pdf` (2023 internship report): six satellite-based indicator panels, Sidr and Aila proxy-versus-prediction comparisons, and mapped 2023 research outputs. Some source PDF figures were physically split into stacked image fragments; the exported assets reconstruct the full original figure rather than presenting incomplete halves.

The ongoing Padma Bridge transport research page uses figures from the supplied working draft: methodology, study area, ferry routes, Monte Carlo counterfactual uncertainty and 2054 conditional scenarios. The unpublished source document is not included in this website package. No ongoing or incomplete qualification is mentioned.

All figure captions specify context and avoid treating forecasts or remote-sensing proxies as measurements. Each figure opens at its native export size. No new research measurements or empirical images have been generated.

To deploy: copy the CONTENTS of `rifatht.github.io` into the root of your existing GitHub Pages repository, commit, and push. Do not copy another `.git` folder or leave the website nested one directory below repository root.
