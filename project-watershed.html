GIS HYDROLOGY COURSEWORK · BANDARBAN

Drainage network and catchment delineation in Bandarban Municipality

A terrain-based GIS study of natural drainage, stream order, pour points and catchments in an undulating urban municipality.

Methods: DEM hydrology · stream order · catchmentsContext: municipal drainage planning
On this page
Planning problemWorkflowOutputsLimitations

Planning problem

Urban drainage follows terrain rather than administrative boundaries. The coursework therefore used elevation to identify likely surface-flow paths, stream hierarchy, outlets and contributing catchments.

GIS workflow

01Prepare terrain surface
02Calculate flow direction & accumulation
03Extract drainage & stream order
04Delineate pour points & catchments

Selected spatial outputs

Limitations

The work is primarily a terrain-based GIS hydrology exercise. Rainfall over catchment area is not the same as runoff, and urban drainage is also affected by land cover, infiltration, roads, culverts and constructed channels. Those factors would need to be added for a fuller drainage-design or flood-resilience study.
